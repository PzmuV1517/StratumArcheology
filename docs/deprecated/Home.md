## What is STRATUM? 🤔

**STRATUM** (Latin for "layer") is a comprehensive digital platform designed specifically for archaeologists who need to:

- 📝 **Plan expeditions** with structured project management
- 🔍 **Document findings** during excavations with digital notes
- 📸 **Capture evidence** with photo attachments and QR-coded artifacts
- 👥 **Collaborate in real-time** with team members
- 📊 **Export data** in standardized formats for analysis

### The Problem We're Solving

Archaeologists currently rely on paper-based note-taking systems that are:
- ❌ Unorganized and difficult to search
- ❌ Prone to loss or damage in field conditions
- ❌ Hard to share with team members
- ❌ Not standardized across projects
- ❌ Difficult to integrate with digital analysis tools

### Our Solution ✨

STRATUM provides a **unified digital suite** that streamlines archaeological workflows through:
- ✅ Structured, searchable documentation
- ✅ Cloud backup with offline sync capabilities
- ✅ Role-based team collaboration
- ✅ Multi-format file support (images, PDFs, CSVs, 3D models)
- ✅ QR-coded artifact tracking
- ✅ Standardized data export
